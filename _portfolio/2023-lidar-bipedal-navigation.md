Built a LiDAR-based autonomous navigation system for a bipedal robot in **ROS 2**, integrating an open-source online mapping module into the team's existing **SLAM** stack.

Bipedal locomotion makes for a noisy sensor platform — the body pitches and rolls with every step — which is what makes the mapping and localization side of this harder than the equivalent wheeled problem.

*Skyland Innovation, HKCRC, Dongguan — Summer 2023*
