I am a robotics software engineer based in Taiwan, working across the full stack that gets a robot to perceive, estimate, and act — visual-inertial state estimation, teleoperation and inverse kinematics, imitation learning, and the embedded systems underneath it all.

Most recently I was at [Dimeidos]({{ base_path }}/cv/) in Kaohsiung, where I worked on 7-DOF teleoperation with swivel-angle constraints and extended [ACT](https://github.com/tonyzhaozh/act) for bimanual imitation learning. Before that, at Aiseed Technology in Taipei, I deployed OpenVINS on a Jetson Nano to fly a PX4-based drone autonomously in GNSS-denied environments.

I hold a BEng in Electronic and Computer Engineering with a minor in Robotics from [HKUST](https://hkust.edu.hk).

What I work on
======
* **Perception & state estimation** — visual-inertial odometry (OpenVINS, VINS-Fusion), EKF, ICP, FAST-LIO2, ORB-SLAM3
* **Robot learning** — imitation learning with ACT and Diffusion Policy, VLA models, PyTorch and LeRobot
* **Teleoperation & control** — inverse kinematics in PyBullet, 7-DOF arm posture optimization, motion planning
* **Embedded & systems** — STM32, FreeRTOS, motor control and ESC firmware (AM32), PX4, ROS 2, Linux, Docker
